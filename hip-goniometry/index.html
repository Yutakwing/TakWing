How to play

  1. Place the axis over the greater trochanter.
  2. Align the stationary arm with the lateral midline of the pelvis.
  3. Align the moving arm with the lateral midline of the femur towards the lateral epicondyle.

Practice note: stabilise the pelvis and avoid lumbar or pelvic compensation. Flex the knee during hip-flexion measurement and keep the knee extended during hip-extension measurement.
