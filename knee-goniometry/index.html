How to play

  1. Place the axis over the lateral femoral epicondyle.
  2. Align the stationary arm with the lateral midline of the femur towards the greater trochanter.
  3. Align the moving arm with the lateral midline of the fibula towards the lateral malleolus.

Practice note: keep the hip in neutral rotation and the thigh supported on the plinth throughout knee flexion.
