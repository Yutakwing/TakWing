Exploring physiotherapy students’ clinical experiences during educational disruption, through a community of practice lens

Williams, T-L., Boggenpoel, B., Hess, D., Steyl, T., Gamiet, S., Madasa, V., Yu, T. W., Mlenzana, N., Karachi, F., & Phillips, J. (2026). Exploring physiotherapy students’ clinical experiences during educational disruption, through a community of practice lens. Health SA Gesondheid, 31, a3286. https://doi.org/10.4102/hsag.v31i0.3286

透過訪談九名物理治療應屆畢業生，探討疫情擾亂下的臨床學習。研究以實踐社群為視角，呈現學系支援、溝通及導師指導的重要性。

Virtual Reality in Health Professions Education: Qualitative Descriptive Study of Educators’ Perspectives

Yu, T. W., Rowe, M., & Frantz, J. (2026). Virtual Reality in Health Professions Education: Qualitative Descriptive Study of Educators’ Perspectives. JMIR XR and Spatial Computing, 3, e52925. https://doi.org/10.2196/52925

此項質性描述研究探討健康專業教育工作者如何看待沉浸式虛擬實境。研究指出，成功實施有賴課程配合、學習支架與院校支援，而不只是硬件。

Perceptions of health professions students on the use of immersive virtual reality

Yu, T. W., Frantz, J., & Rowe, M. (2024). Perceptions of health professions students on the use of immersive virtual reality. African Journal of Health Professions Education, 16(2), e1143. https://doi.org/10.7196/AJHPE.2024.v16i2.1143

這項質性研究探討18名健康專業學生使用沉浸式虛擬實境的經驗。學生描述投入程度，以及在解剖和臨床教育中的應用潛力，同時指出技術與財務挑戰。這些是學生觀感，並非經量度的學習成效。

Exercise therapy education enriched through interprofessional teaching

Yu, T. W., Achmat, G., Kock, L., & Smithdorf, G. (2021). Exercise therapy education enriched through interprofessional teaching. Medical Education, 55(11), 1308-1309. https://doi.org/10.1111/medu.14619

Participation restrictions and vocational rehabilitation needs experienced by persons with a unilateral lower limb amputation in the Western Cape, South Africa

Yu, T. W., & Ennion, L. (2019). Participation restrictions and vocational rehabilitation needs experienced by persons with a unilateral lower limb amputation in the Western Cape, South Africa. African Journal of Disability, 8, a456. https://doi.org/10.4102/ajod.v8i0.456

此研究探討南非西開普省單側下肢截肢人士面對的參與限制與職業復康需要。結果突顯重要的活動障礙，並倡議更能支援重返工作及社會參與的復康服務。
