AI, Virtual Reality, Clinical Reasoning, and Health Professions Education

I am a physiotherapy educator and researcher at Saint Francis University in Hong Kong. My work focuses on health professions education, particularly the purposeful design and implementation of artificial intelligence, virtual reality, educational technology, assessment, and clinical-reasoning activities.

My research and teaching are grounded in a simple principle: technology should address a genuine educational need rather than be introduced for novelty alone. I am particularly interested in curriculum alignment, learning design, accessibility, implementation, student engagement, and the transfer of learning into professional practice.

This website combines my academic portfolio with a public notebook in which I reflect on teaching, learning, research, academic work, and emerging technology.
