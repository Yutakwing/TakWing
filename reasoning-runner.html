Why this game?

Artificial intelligence can support learning, but confident answers are not always correct. Safe clinical reasoning requires evidence, context, reflection and recognition of red flags.

Learning activity

This mini-game is for reflection and discussion. It is not a validated assessment, does not measure clinical competence and should not be used for grading or research. Your best score remains in this browser; only anonymous page views, starts, and completions are counted centrally.
