How to play

  1. Place the axis over the lateral aspect of the humeral head.
  2. Align the stationary arm parallel to the mid-axillary line.
  3. Align the moving arm with the lateral midline of the humerus towards the lateral epicondyle.

Practice note: keep the trunk upright and avoid lumbar extension, lateral lean or shoulder abduction. Keep the elbow straight and the forearm neutral throughout the measurement.
