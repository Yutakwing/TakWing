How to play

  1. Place the axis just inferior to the lateral malleolus.
  2. Align the stationary arm with the lateral midline of the fibula towards the fibular head.
  3. Align the moving arm parallel to the lateral aspect of the fifth metatarsal.

Practice note: stabilise the lower leg and avoid substituting movement through the midfoot.
