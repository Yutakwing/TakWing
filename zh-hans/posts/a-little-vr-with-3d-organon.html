以 3D Organon 探索虚拟现实

记录使用 3D Organon 探索解剖学与沉浸式学习的初步经验。

文章正文目前保留英文原文，以确保学术内容的准确性。标题、摘要及网站界面已提供简体中文版本。

So this year, while I was at the AMEE 2023 Conference in Glasgow, I managed to get some action with the Occulus linked to 3D organon.

I’ve never played with 3D Organon since it’s a rather pricey software (ranging from $199 to $499). However, I have played with Sharecare VR anatomy. Which I guess you can say is similar but much cheaper.

I’m getting a sponsored version of 3D Organon from the organisers themselves. Hopefully, I can compare the similarities and differences between these two applications.

Keep a look out on this space, I’ll definitely start testing soon!
