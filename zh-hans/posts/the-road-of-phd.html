健康专业教育 · 2020年5月21日

博士研究之路

反思博士研究历程中的挑战、指导关系与持续学习。

文章正文目前保留英文原文，以确保学术内容的准确性。标题、摘要及网站界面已提供简体中文版本。

Hi, how are you doing? I have written in a while, so I thought I’ll start now. Yes, I don’t want to do my work, so I’m going to do something that is somewhat work enhancing, which is… writing.

I just wanted to write something, and this will probably end up being very short. When people say the road of the PhD is very long and lonely, I thought, arg, whatever, it’s hate talk etc. But now that I am in it, I am thinking it is a lonely road, and it feels like I’m writing and writing like pore water into a toilet, I don’t know how much or when it’ll be full.

I won’t say it’s lonely, but it’s a lost journey. In comparison to a Masters, there is even less structure, even less guidelines. It is like the supervisor saying, go explore and do what you feel your heart desires. But, we will regulate you if you go over board. These regulations will shatter your ideas and goals. Not because they are not good enough, it is because they are too good, it’s like a dream, an un-graduatable dream.

“Lets get the degree over and done with, then you can start with your dream-research.” I understand that, I started off with a big project, and now, it is really small, somewhat linked to what I want to do, but not quite. Nevertheless, I will keep moving. I mean, let me keep moving…

See you around…
