Professional learning

Courses & Workshops

Research-informed professional learning for health professions education and allied health practice.

My courses and workshops translate questions emerging from teaching, research and educational development into practical professional learning.

The emphasis is not on adopting technology for novelty. Each programme begins with a meaningful educational or clinical problem, examines where technology may add value, and considers how the resulting approach can be evaluated responsibly.

Programmes may be offered as short courses, workshops, invited faculty-development sessions or collaborative pilots.
