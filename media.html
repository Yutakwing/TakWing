Publishing standard

Useful before impressive

Media will be published only when it adds something that text alone cannot show clearly.

01

Context included

Every item will explain the educational problem, audience, and purpose behind it.

02

Accessible by design

Videos and audio will include captions or transcripts; presentation files will include readable supporting notes.

03

Evidence made visible

Claims, limitations, and links to relevant research will travel with the media.

04

Consent and rights respected

Student work, photographs, recordings, and slides will appear only when permissions and reuse rights are clear.
