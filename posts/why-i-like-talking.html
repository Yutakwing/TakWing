Why I like talking

I am an extrovert, so they say. Well, I do admit that I get energy by doing fun and stressful things. It was made quite clear to me, I was the MC of my brothers wedding, oh boy, did I enjoy that. The busy-ness, behind the scene organizing, setting up, solving possible problems before it happens.

I actually wanted to write about something more serious, but for today, let me just settle with something simple. So I have been told many times, that those that talk much are those who do little, I may disagree with this, because I think it refers to the situation. If it’s in a meeting, maybe that is correct, but if you’re referring to a personal character, then it might not all be correct.

Talking helps me understand and figure things out, like some staying in a room and internally digesting something, I prefer speaking to people about it. You can imagine how my PhD supervisor will hate me right? Every time I have an idea, I would just want to go crazy and chat to them about it. That is a joke, because both my supervisors are professors, and they are indeed, quite busy.

So, why do I like talking? I personally think it is because it is how humans are different from others. And we should communicate to ensure there is no miss understanding. I talk to anyone, anytime and anywhere. I once watched a series where a casual citizen wanted to apply for a job, and needed to be a negotiator. This negotiator was a profession that worked with the police force to speak to criminals, psychological patients, mentally unstable citizens.

In one of the scenes, during he’s interview, he was required to speak about a watch. Although he knew nothing about a watch, the was able to speak about it, and link that to many other new topics, which eventually got him the job. I of course am not apply for any job, but I admire the ability to keep conversations going, and I think it is important for us to be able to do so. Whether it is professionally or personally. And the only way of getting this write is by talking, getting ideas and catching on what the person is saying and find their interest to spark more conversation.

If you have the ability to be able to spark up conversations, it’s better than struggling to think of something when it happens. Nevertheless, I just want to be able to make conversation with every one that I come across, as I feel there is a story that one wants to talk about in everyone.
