Discovery Product Review Two

Original WordPress post

Let me continue with this chapter. I think it is important to thinking about what you really want regarding this membership.

The additional benefits you can get is the money back counter. You can choose from a various amount of stores like, Woolworth, Pick ‘n Pay, Total Sports, Sportsman warehouse, etc. It is important to see where you spend your money, because once you select, you cannot change it. Ie: If you shop more at Pick ‘n Pay, don’t choose Woolworth, because the percentage of return is different.

Lastly, the flight, hotel and car rentals. There is some discount here, of course, it’s not the best, but it’s not bad on good timing deals. Although, the booking cannot be simultaneous. Meaning you cannot book a car for today and expect a discount. I cannot remember how long it must be prior your bookings, so it’s best to figure that out and plan ahead. Although sometimes it’s cheaper booking one way with the discounted flights from Discovery, and another (return) from another company, like SAFair.

iWatch, Shoe boaster and watch boasters. Of course being with discovery, you would want to purchase your apple or non apple sport watch. What is nice is that Discovery allows you to buy any of them, what you have to do is pay up front, and then Discovery will pay you every month, back. You have to review your status to see how much you limit is, IE: they don’t pay more than R2500.00, so if you buy a 6k watch, they only going to pay you 2.5k back (well, 2.5 / 12 = 208 pm). So before you sink in to pay that, you must be careful.

Additionally, you don’t have to buy the watch, but you can get a pair of shoes. This is also something you have to work out. What is the maxim price of the shoe you can get. If I have the time, I will list this is my next blog post, to make things easier.

If you’re planning to get the shoe and an non-apple sport watch, sad news for you, you cannot. You can only have one boaster activated at a time. With that being said, there are different categories of boasters. There are four boaster categories, Bike Boaster, non-apple watch and shoe boaster, apple watch boaster and gym boaster. These above mentioned four, you can have simultaneously. For example, I have Shoe, apple watch and gym boaster active all at once. If I cycled, I would have used that boaster too, but sadly, I don’t.

Apple watches. I know they are VERY expensive, but it’s cheap with discovery. But they bind you to a credit card, which means more payments. Eg: bank fees and monthly costs. But of course it still works out cheaper than buying it on contract or cashing it out. I personally have also shifted to discovery banking, and car insure (Which I will also write about soon).

Now, if you activated all these boasters, how do you make sure you don’t pay this back. It’s actually very simple, you need to ensure you reach all your goals. How do you reach your goals? Well, it depends how active you are. Your active goals goes as follows:

*60% heart rate for an hour gets you 100 points

*70% of your heart rate for 30 minutes gets you 200 points

*70% of your heart rate for an hour gets you 300 points

*80% of your heart rate gets you 300 points

How do you work out your maximum heart rate? Take (220 – your age) x 0.80. So for example: I am 30, 220 – 30 = 190 x 0.8 = 152 beats per minutes. Meaning to get 300 points I must get an average of 152 beats per minute for a period of 30 minutes to get my 300 points.

Getting 152 is a little impossible for you? Well, stay connected, I’ll tell you the ways you can get points!
