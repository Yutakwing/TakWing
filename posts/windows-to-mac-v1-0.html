Hi all, so today I will talk about what the feeling was when moving from Windows to Mac.

I have been using Windows for all of my life. I was one of those who always spoke bad about apple, and not used it. I know all the short cuts on windows, the way it works and function etc. But the one down thing about windows is that, the laptop needs replacement, or formatting ever so often.

And, when you’re a junior, you have all the time to do so. Changing parts, troubleshooting which component of the computer is not working. But as time goes by, you just need your technology to work. Life is stressful enough already, and a not working PC, is worse.

I have been using apple, since 2015, and today, I feel like an apple collector. I won’t talk good about apple, and I won’t talk good about window either. I am here today to speak about the feeling of transit that I went through from going window to mac.

At first, frustration, anger, unhappiness, tricked and deceived. Before anything experiences (like a Mac book Air) you would read reviews. I read good and bad reviews, but none talks about the transfer from window to mac. And now, I’m going to talk about.

It took me almost a month to get used to the function and shortcuts. First things first, the mac is beautiful, but is it good in function?

(1) the keyboard layout is different. Control, alt and windows key, are all relocated to another location. My pinky being a strong finger from all the control-ing is now just shifting. I guess the point of mac is meaningful, as our thumb is stronger than our pinky. Nevertheless, it took some time to get used to the keyboard. Then the short cut, the short cuts concepts are the same, but the keys are different. The one that took me the longest to figure out was “delete” and “copy and pasting”.

Moving on, there is no more “menu” where all your software is stored. So everything are “apps” now, no longer having software. Of course the concept is still the same, you install software like how you install “apps” on your phone.

SPACE! Space is limited and very expensive, I had to buy an expansion SD card for this (which is another conversation, but have a look, this is what I bought for my Mac Book Air). I really struggled with space, moving from an 1TB SSD to a 128 SSD, okay, the speed was better, but it took sometime to get used to the fact that you can no longer just “keep things” around. High capacity items like movies and pictures, I had to remove or move to the SD Card. I guess you could carry a hard drive to assist with the space issue, but being a apple person, the whole point is actually minimalist (which you will soon realize, it’s not true.), well to a certain extent.

Next, software and drivers. It took me forever to setup my printer, because I couldn’t, or better yet, don’t know how to find the drive for Mac and install it. And no, I’m not computer literate. Anyhow, it worked at the end, but you really would struggle if you don’t have the time to figure all of this out.

Desktop, desktop and desktop. This is where I think it’s debatable, apple has many “desktops” where windows only have one, with many “blocks” where you can drag and it sits. Apple makes it easier for you to multitask by having multiple desktops that you have swipe left and right, which is nice, until you need to read and write, this is where window excel. Yes, yes, I know that Mac can have split window, but it’s easier on a window PC than a Apple. But there is a work around, you can install a third party app (which again takes time to read up and figure out).
