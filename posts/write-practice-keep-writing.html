Hi,

How are you doing? Giving the current time, I think it’s a good and a bad time. My one friend once told me, in order to get better that something, you have to practice it.

Therefore, I’m going to practice writing, by writing. You may ask, what are you going to write about? The brilliant thing is that I don’t know what I will be writing until I write about it.

Today, I will speak about the hero that fought for us during this pandemic, we understand that everyone has duties and responsibilities, but its not always that persons destiny to walk away from those duties and responsibilities. I therefore would like to thanks all those who has fought for us, so that we can stay at home and be safe with our families.

According to what I know, today is a day of remembrance. Meaning we think about about our loved ones, who are no longer with us anymore. Our family, friends and comrades. We pray to our Gods that their trip will go safe and smooth, and that our future will progress and prosperous.

I hope everyone remains safe during this difficult time. Please stay in doors to prevent the spread. It is a difficult time, but if we get through this, we will see the sunshine again.
