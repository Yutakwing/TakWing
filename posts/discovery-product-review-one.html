Discovery has been around for many years, and of guilt, I have not been to active regarding using their products. Their apple watch with active goals has been around since 2015?

Disclaimer:
Before I continue, please note that Discovery has not paid me to do any of this, I am just doing this for the sake of writing and telling the whole the experience that I had by using their products, so whether it’s a good or bad experience it is not because someone asked or paid me to do it.

I signed up for Discovery’s product sign March 2019, by the end of October, I changed my car insurance, bank and signed up for their gym. To be honest, I don’t really know whether to start, but like all games, we have to start somewhere, let’s talk about one of the more well known items on the list: Discovery and Gym membership.

Discovery and Virgin Active:

So, you first need to be part of their Discovery Medical Aid program to be able to activate this benefit. There is an activation fee involved, and like all things, we have a heat period of 3 minutes, where we will be frantic about this “new product” and then it becomes “boring”. So before signing for gym, yes, I know its very exciting and it “motivates” you, but if you don’t have a habit of going to gym or exercising before signing, then you probably shouldn’t. Nevertheless, I signed up only to realize that it bonds you to a year contract, meaning any cancellation to policy will result in fines.

Also, if you have done your research, there are different packages that you can sign up for. (1) off peak, (2) All day, one gym, (3) anywhere any time. Obviously the cost changes based on the package you choose. For me? I chose (3) anywhere anytime, because I could go shower in any gym, work out based on where I worked etc. It was all fun, soon to realize that gym actually takes a lot of time, a 30 minute session (required by discovery) actually takes about an hour.

What do I mean?
Well, you get to gym, park, get changed and then warm up + gym (40-45 minutes) then cool down (5 minutes) and then you have about 10 minutes to shower and get ready. Altogether takes about an hour. It’s all good if you don’t have to go anywhere, you just pack your things and go home. But what I’m saying is, there is no “quick” workout. Additionally, you still have the issue of “gym fear”, meaning fear to do something because you think someone is watching you.
