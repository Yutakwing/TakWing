Hello there,

This is going to be a short post. I realize everyone has their own time for epiphanies. Mine to this, was today.

If you ask the big question “how can we become successful?”. Many would reply: KNOWLEDGE, some would say HARD-WORK. But what if none of these gives you success?

Does not make sense right? How can neither KNOWLEDGE nor HARD-WORK result in success? The answer is simple. Let us play a game, if each letter of the alphabet is replaced with a number that represents its position, eg: A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, etc…

KNOWLEDGE then becomes (11+14+15+23+12+5+4+7+5) = 96%
HARD-WORK then becomes (8+1+18+4+23+15+18+11) = 98%

Interestingly, neither of them gives you 100%, meanwhile if ATTITUDE is spelt out the same way…

ATTITUDE becoming (1+20+20+9+20+21+4+5) = 100%.

Not sure if this is a coincidences or not, but having the right attitude to start off and finish with is important. That is one of the biggest determining factor. Of course, I think having knowledge and being a hard-worker also contributes to being successful, but more importantly, having a positive and strong attitude is important. In this case, more important.

Keep well, and be strong.
