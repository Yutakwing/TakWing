My Experience with the Alienware M15 R2 and HTC VIVE COSMO Setup

In 2020, I had the incredible opportunity to acquire the Alienware M15 R2 laptop, a beast in its time and arguably even now. Although the gaming world has evolved rapidly since then, especially with powerhouses like the ROG ALLY, some might argue that the Alienware M15 R2 has become somewhat dated. But let’s dive into the specifics.

Specifications:

  • Processor: Intel i7 9th-9750H Gen @ 2.6Ghz
  • RAM: 16GB
  • Storage: SSD 256GB
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 2070

For this venture, I paired my laptop with the HTC VIVE COSMO – a potent combo for any VR enthusiast.

But it’s not all about the hardware; the setup process deserves its fair share of spotlight. The initial step, which required a mini-display port, proved a challenge. Adapters for such aren’t just readily available in SA; you’d likely have to order one, and waiting can be rather tiresome.

Once all the gadgets were in hand, the real setup began. Excitement bubbling, I dove into the world of VR, only to be told there was too much light or the visuals needed tweaking. Patience truly is a virtue here.

Breaking down the setup time:

  • Software Installation: Roughly 2 hours.
  • Physical Environment Preparation: Approximately 15 minutes. (A necessary safety measure to avoid any potential mishaps.)
  • HTC VIVE Virtual Environment Configuration: About 30 minutes.

Of course, don’t forget about game and application downloads, which can vary based on your internet speed.

Summing it all up, the entire setup took me around 3.5 hours. Quite the commitment, but was it worth it? Absolutely. However, now I’m curious – how does this setup time compare to the Oculus? Lucky for me (and you, dear readers), I’ve got one on hand. Stay tuned for that comparison in a future post!
