Student Skills Lab privacy notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026. This notice describes the account and progress system in the repository. It is separate from public-site analytics.

Information recorded

Allocated username, account status, a salted password hash and session records are stored for authentication. Plaintext passwords are not stored in the database. A session token is held in your browser’s sessionStorage for sign-in; sessions expire after eight hours or are revoked when you log out.

Tracked activities record the activity ID, attempt identifier, technical and independence scores, completion, attempts within the activity, hint and AI request counts, highest support level, duration, categorised errors, timestamps and limited activity metrics such as typing speed and accuracy. A separate progress summary records latest/best scores and cumulative attempts and support use. The result endpoint does not store chatbot transcripts or raw pointer movements.

Purpose and assessment

Records support practice feedback, personal progress and educational improvement. Scores are intended for formative practice and do not represent formal clinical competency assessment. These records are not a certification of competence or an automatic summative grade. Public, untracked play remains available without saving results to a student account.

Who can access records

Signed-in students can retrieve only their own progress and recent attempts. The system operator and authorised administrators with Cloudflare database access can access stored records. There is no public student leaderboard or lecturer analytics dashboard in this system. The institution must confirm the named staff authorised for administrative access.

Infrastructure and optional AI services

GitHub Pages hosts the frontend; a Cloudflare Worker handles authentication and Cloudflare D1 stores account and result records. If configured by the operator, a completed result can be forwarded to a Zapier webhook with a pseudonymous user reference, not a name, email, password or session token. Repository inspection cannot confirm whether this optional forwarding is enabled in production.

The optional Zapier AI Tutor is a separate external service. Messages you enter and game context you choose to share are sent to that service. Do not enter patient details or other confidential information. Tutor conversations may be handled under the provider’s policies and configuration; they are not stored as transcripts by the D1 result endpoint. Anonymous activity analytics are separate from student-account progress: they record random event/run identifiers, activity, page view/start/hint/completion events, stage where applicable, duration and timestamp. They do not store usernames or session tokens.

Retention

No formal retention period or automated deletion schedule for account and learning records is defined in the repository. An institutional decision is required, including arrangements for account closure, deletion and backup retention. Expired sessions are cleaned up by the API; this does not delete learning records.

Research use

Educational records do not automatically become research data. Any research use requires the appropriate institutional and ethical processes, including information and consent where applicable.

Questions, access and corrections

Contact twyu@sfu.edu.hk to enquire about your records, request a correction or discuss access or deletion. Requests will need identity verification and review under the applicable institutional arrangements; the website does not provide an automatic deletion service.
