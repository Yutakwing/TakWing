What does the lab explore?

Clinical readiness is more than recalling information. The stations ask you to challenge confident claims, align technology with learning outcomes and act safely when a scenario changes.

Learning activity

This mini-game supports reflection and discussion. It is not a validated assessment, does not measure clinical competence and should not be used for grading or research. Anonymous page views, starts, and completions are counted; no answers or personal details are uploaded.
