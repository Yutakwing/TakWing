How to play

  1. Place the axis over the olecranon process.
  2. Align the stationary arm perpendicular to the floor.
  3. Align the moving arm with the ulna towards the ulnar styloid.

Practice note: maintain 90° shoulder abduction and 90° elbow flexion, support the humerus, stabilise the scapula and do not force the range.
