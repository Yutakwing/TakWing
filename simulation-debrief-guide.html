Teaching resource

Simulation debrief: from observation to the next attempt

An adaptable discussion worksheet based on my simulation teaching reflection. It is a practical teaching aid, not a validated assessment instrument.

Use during the human debrief

  1. Notice

    What happened? Describe one observable action without naming a learner or patient.

  2. Explain

    What reasoning informed that action? Ask the learner before offering your interpretation.

  3. Plan the next attempt

    Choose one strength to retain and one change to try. What would show that the change helped?

Focus the discussion

Consider preparation and flow; the patient’s priorities; focused examination and reasoning; treatment selection; safety, monitoring and reassessment; and communication and teamwork. Select what fits the activity rather than turning every theme into a checklist score.

If AI helps consolidate notes afterwards

Use only approved systems and appropriately de-identified material. Keep observation separate from interpretation, check the summary against the original discussion, and retain educator responsibility for feedback and judgement. This page does not collect responses or send them to an AI service.
